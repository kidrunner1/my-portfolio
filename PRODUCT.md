# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The primary audience is HR teams, companies hiring Front-End Developers, freelance clients, and startup teams looking for a developer who can turn ideas into polished web experiences. They arrive with limited time, so the site must quickly communicate capability, reliability, work quality, and collaboration fit.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio exists to present Krit Daowaset as a Front-End Developer with strength in Next.js, React, TypeScript, UX/UI thinking, performance, and maintainable implementation. Success means visitors can understand the developer's strengths within seconds, inspect credible project work, and confidently start a conversation through the contact page.

## Brand Personality

Reliable, modern, and UX-minded. The voice should feel professional without becoming stiff: confident, clear, approachable, and focused on real outcomes. The experience should make the work feel production-ready, thoughtful, and easy to trust.

## Anti-references

Avoid looking like a generic portfolio template, a cluttered showcase, or a rigid job-application page. Do not let the design become overly loud, gimmicky, or dominated by harsh saturated colors. Avoid decorative effects that distract from the work, excessive card stacking, vague copy, and layouts that make the visitor hunt for evidence.

## Design Principles

Lead with proof, not decoration: every section should help the visitor understand skill, process, or project value.

Make the first scan effortless: key strengths, role, project categories, and contact paths should be obvious without heavy reading.

Balance polish with clarity: visual craft should support trust and usability, not compete with the portfolio content.

Show production mindset: performance, responsive behavior, accessibility, and maintainable code are part of the brand promise.

Keep the tone human: the site should feel like a capable developer inviting real collaboration, not a faceless resume.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Preserve strong text contrast, keyboard-accessible navigation and controls, semantic page structure, responsive behavior across mobile and desktop, and clear focus states. Motion and animation should respect `prefers-reduced-motion` so users who request reduced motion still get a calm, readable experience.
